LexiDecay v2
Graph-based text classification you can actually explain

No neural networks. No GPU. No embeddings. LexiDecay builds a statistical co-occurrence graph from your training text and returns full, human-readable evidence for every prediction — from spam filtering and intent detection to custom semantic classifiers.

HIGHLIGHTS

Why LexiDecay?

Six reasons it stands apart from black-box classifiers.

Fully explainable

Every prediction traces back to graph nodes and evidence types — no black box.

CPU-only, no embeddings

Runs on any machine; training speed is close to logistic regression on TF-IDF.

Rigorous evaluation

Reports Spam F1, Ham F1 and Macro F1 with StratifiedKFold k=5, not a single lucky split.

Online learning

partial_fit() updates the graph incrementally without retraining from scratch.

Structural features

Regex pseudo-tokens like __MONEY__ capture patterns with zero manual feature engineering.

Apache 2.0 licensed

Commercially permissive — free to use in personal and enterprise projects alike.

PIPELINE

How it works, in five steps

01

Build the relation graph

Tokens and co-occurrences become weighted nodes and edges; discriminativeness disc(v,c)=log(P(v|c)/P(v)) is computed for every node.

02

Discover phrases

Bigrams and trigrams passing G² > 10.83 (p < 0.001) and NPMI > 0.3 become independent phrase nodes.

03

Extract structural features

Regex patterns inject pseudo-tokens like __LONG_NUM__ before tokenization even begins.

04

Aggregate five sources of evidence

Direct, phrase, context, propagation (bounded PPR) and interaction evidence are weighted and summed.

05

Apply a calibrated threshold

The decision boundary that maximizes spam F1 on training data is stored and reused at inference.
